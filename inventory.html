How to Connect Your Google Sheet (2 Minutes)
  1. Open a Google Sheet → click Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. Delete any existing code, click Copy Apps Script above, and paste it in.
  3. Click Deploy → New deployment → Type: Web app.
  4. Set Execute as: Me, and Who has access: Anyone, then click Deploy.
  5. Copy the generated .../exec URL and paste it above!
